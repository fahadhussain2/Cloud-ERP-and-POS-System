## Goal

**The cashier sees each cash bill within seconds of its finalize, grouped by salesman, and confirms it with Enter; when the internet is down, scanning the printed bill records it just as well.** The cash desk opens a **cash session** with its float; the **live queue** shows the branch's cash bills awaiting handover and refunds awaiting payout; Enter (or a scan, which jumps to the bill) writes a **handover document** in the desk's own outbox, local-first like a bill; the cashier can correct the salesman's cash received, and the correction is kept on the handover, never on the bill. Offline, a scan records the handover from the bill number and amount in its code, and the server matches it when the counter's bill arrives. Every handover posts `Dr CASH_DRAWER / Cr CASH_IN_TRANSIT`, so cash in transit falls back to zero as bills are handed over.

## Design

| Choice | Why |
|---|---|
| **The cash desk is the same POS app** on a device of kind `cash_desk` (`D01`), which already gets its kind with its token (platform spec §1). After unlock, someone holding `cash.handover.receive` lands on the **Cash desk** screen; **`Alt+Q`** switches between it and selling (the desk also sells, D-001). On a counter (`C…`) the screen doesn't exist. | One app, one local database, one push. `F6` and `Alt+D` belong to Chrome (POS spec §11); `Alt+Q` is free. |
| **Cash session** (CSH-01): *Open session* asks for the **opening float** (whole rupees, may be 0). One open session per desk; it belongs to the cashier who opened it. Another cashier who unlocks sees *Session of Ali open since 10:02* and can only sell until it is closed. **Close session** (`cash.session.close`) shows the expected cash (float + handovers − payouts) and closes it, so the next cashier can open theirs (owner answer 1). The denomination count, variance and acknowledgement come with W1-11 (CSH-07). | PRD CSH-01: "belongs to one cash desk device and cashier; can hand over to another cashier (close/open)". Until W1-11, the drawer is counted the legacy way (D-090). |
| **The session open and close are documents of family `Z`** (`CashSessionV1`, `event: 'open' \| 'close'`), numbered `D01-Z000001`, made in one local transaction with their outbox row (rule 6). The server keeps one `cash_sessions` row: inserted by the open, its close fields set by the close (workflow fields, audited). **The float posts nothing:** it is cash already in the drawer, the base of the expected cash. | Architecture §4.4 gives `Z` to the session; a session must exist offline, so it can't be a server-made row. The float moving from the safe is a cash movement (`M`, W1-11). |
| **A new pull dataset `cash_queue`** (sync spec §3), pulled only by cash desk devices: table `sales_documents`, **the device's branch, `cash_due ≠ 0`, and pending or received in the last 7 days**. Columns: `id, number, device_id, salesperson_user_id, created_at_device, business_date, cash_due, cash_received, change_due, handover_status, status, row_version, deleted_at`. Migration `0011` adds `cash_received` and `change_due` to `sales_documents` (filled once by push from the cash payment, backfilled from `sales_payments`), gives `sales_documents` the sync notify trigger, and the queue is ordered by `created_at_device`. **No lines, no customer.** | PRD W4.1: counter, bill number, time, amount due, cash received and change. A received bill leaves the queue because its `handover_status` change is a new `txid` (rule 8). The 7 days keep the first pull small and a forgotten bill visible. |
| **`queue-changed`** (sync spec §8): a change to `sales_documents` notifies on its own channel with the tenant and branch; the API sends `queue-changed` only to that branch's **cash desk** streams (counters get nothing), throttled like `changes-available`. The desk pulls `cash_queue` on it. **Target: finalize to visible ≤ 5 s** (architecture §4.6), measured in the e2e and on staging. | SYN-04. Counters don't pull the queue, so they shouldn't be woken by every bill. |
| **The queue on screen** (CSH-02, PRD W4): pending bills grouped by salesman (name, then bills newest last), each row: counter, bill number, time, **amount due** (green), cash received, change; **refunds** (`cash_due < 0`) in orange as *Pay out Rs 450*. Totals per salesman. `↑ ↓` selects, **Enter confirms as shown**; **`E`** edits the cash received first (the change follows). A bill confirmed on this desk leaves the queue at once (the local handover hides it before the server replies). The queue panel scrolls inside itself; the session bar and the scan field never move (POS-17). | PRD W4.2: "presses Enter to confirm. No scan is needed." A correction is rare, so it gets its own key and doesn't slow the normal Enter. |
| **The handover check is pure, in `packages/domain`** (`handover.ts`, rule 2): `checkHandover({ session, cashier, entry, existing })` → the first problem, an i18n key `handover.<code>`: `no_session`, `not_session_cashier`, `not_permitted` (`cash.handover.receive` for a bill, `cash.refund.pay` for a refund), `already_handed_over` (with when, who and the handover number), `nothing_to_collect` (a code with amount 0: *Paid without cash*), `received_short` (a corrected cash received below the amount due). `changeGiven = cashReceived − amountDue` (0 when cash received is empty = exact). | One rule set for the desk and the server's review; golden cases first. |
| **The handover document** (`CashHandoverV1`, family `H`, `buildHandoverDocument`): `id` (UUIDv7), `seq`, `number` (`D01-H000001`), `deviceId`, `branchId`, `sessionId`, `kind` (`in` \| `payout`), `billNumber`, `saleDocumentId` (null when the bill isn't known yet), `amount` (the cash due, or the refund as a positive figure: **the posted figure**), `cashReceived`, `changeGiven`, `correctedFrom` (the salesman's cash received, when the cashier changed it), `method` (`queue` \| `scan` \| `auto`), `cashierId`, `createdAt`, `businessDate`, `clientVersion`. Written with its outbox row in **one local transaction**, signed after the commit, pushed at once (like a bill, POS spec §12). | Data model §6 and architecture §4.6: a POS document in the desk's own series; the sale document is never touched (rule 3). |
| **Scan** (CSH-03): the desk's scan field is always focused when no dialog is open; a keyboard-wedge scan ends with Enter and is read with `parseBillCode`. **In the queue** → the bill is selected and Enter confirms it (method `scan`). **Not in the queue** (the counter hasn't synced, or the desk is offline): *C03-S000412 isn't in the queue yet. Record Rs 4,320 from the bill?*, Enter records it with `saleDocumentId` null (owner answer 2). Already handed over → refused with a warning tone and who took it. A refund code records a payout. | PRD W4 offline and W15: the code carries the bill number and amount, so it works before the counter syncs. One confirmation step keeps a stray scan from recording cash. |
| **The bill code gains refunds:** `parseBillCode` accepts a negative amount (`C03-S000415 -450` = a refund of Rs 450); `billCode` prints one for a document with `cash_due < 0` (Code 39 has `-`). Sales are unchanged. | PRD W7: the refund is paid "by scanning the code, e.g. when offline". W1-13 prints the first such bill. |
| **Refund payout** (CSH-04, owner answer 3): the queue and the scan treat `cash_due < 0` as a payout (`kind: payout`, needs `cash.refund.pay`, *Pay out Rs 450* in orange). Built and tested now with return rows made in the tests; **the first real refund comes with returns (W1-13)**, which adds its e2e. | PRD W7: "the reverse of the handover". The desk part is the same code path, so it's cheaper now than reopening the desk in W1-13. |
| **The desk's own cash sales** are handed over **automatically** (PRD W4.5): `finalizeSale` on a desk with an open session (whoever sells) writes the bill and an `H` (method `auto`, the bill's cash due) in the **same local transaction**. With no open session the bill waits in the queue like any other (owner answer 4). | Posting rules §4.5: "sale on the cash desk device itself … plus an automatic handover". A document made by the desk keeps the posting the same as any handover and works offline. |
| **Push handles `H` and `Z`** (sync spec §6.2): the same claim, signature, schema (`CashHandoverV1`, `CashSessionV1`), device and reference checks as a sale; then `reviewHandover` (`packages/domain`) and storing. **Never refused on business rules** (rule 7). | One trust path for every document. |
| **On the server** (`cash_handovers`, `cash_sessions`, migration `0011`, data model §6): a handover whose bill is stored **links it and sets the bill's `handover_status`** (`received` / `paid_out`, audited); one whose bill hasn't arrived is stored with `bill_number` and linked **when the bill is accepted** (the sale's push looks it up). Findings, each an exception: **`handover_amount_mismatch`** (medium, D-059) when the amount differs from the bill's cash due; **`duplicate_handover`** (new, medium) when the bill already has a handover of that kind: stored with `duplicate_of_id`, **not posted**, the bill's status unchanged; **`handover_not_permitted`** (new, low) when the server says the cashier lacks the permission. A unique index on `(tenant_id, bill_number, kind)` where `duplicate_of_id IS NULL`. Every table: `tenant_id` + forced RLS, `enable_sync`, `enable_audit` (rule 5); mirrored in `schema.ts`; the app role can't update or delete a handover. | Data model §6: "a second confirmation of the same bill is reported as a duplicate", matched by bill number so offline scans work. Accepting and flagging keeps the cashier's record (D-011); posting it twice would invent cash. |
| **Posting** (`postHandover` in `packages/posting`, posting rules §4.5): `in` → `Dr CASH_DRAWER / Cr CASH_IN_TRANSIT`, `payout` → the reverse, for `amount`, dated the handover's business date, memo the handover and bill numbers; in the accepting transaction under the posting lock, caught up by the worker, and replayed by the rebuild check (accounting spec §5). Cash received, corrections and change never post. **It posts its own amount**, so its order against the bill doesn't matter; a mismatch is flagged, not adjusted. | Posting rules §4.5: "a handover always posts the bill's cash due"; with an offline scan the code's amount is the bill's cash due. Order-independent postings keep the rebuild exact. |
| **The desk is offline-first:** the status bar's offline state also shows on the queue: *Offline since 14:05 · queue not updating · scan each bill*. The queue it has stays visible (marked stale); confirms and scans keep writing handovers; the outbox and health count `H` and `Z` like bills. | PRD W15: the cash desk still handles handovers and payouts by scanning. |
| **Local schema 8:** `cash_queue` (exactly the pulled columns), `cash_sessions` (this desk's sessions: `id`, `number`, `cashier_user_id`, `opened_at`, `opening_float`, `closed_at`, `closed_by_user_id`, `status`), `local_series` for `H` and `Z`; handovers in `local_documents` as `H`, indexed by bill number for the duplicate check. A counter with open bills, outbox and quarantined rows upgrades in place (rule 9). | Data model §12 (`local_cash_session`). |
| **Settings:** `cash_handover_mode = off` (CSH-09, Wave 2) is not built: a desk on such a tenant shows *Handover confirmation is off* instead of the queue. | Delux is Required (D-049). |

## Scope

1. **`packages/domain`:** `handover.ts` (`checkHandover`, `buildHandoverDocument`, `reviewHandover`, `queueGroups` for the grouping and totals, `expectedCash`), `cash-session.ts` (`buildSessionDocument`), `parseBillCode` / `billCode` with a negative amount; golden `handover.json`, `handover-review.json`, `bill-code.json` cases, `queue.json`.
2. **`packages/contracts`:** `CashHandoverV1`, `CashSessionV1`, push accepting families `H` and `Z`, the `cash_queue` columns and dataset, `duplicate_handover` and `handover_not_permitted` in the exception kinds, the `queue-changed` event.
3. **`packages/db`:** migration `0011_cash_desk.sql` (`cash_sessions`, `cash_handovers`, the two `sales_documents` columns and their backfill, the queue notify trigger, the exception kinds), `schema.ts`.
4. **`packages/posting`:** `postHandover`, its golden cases, the database layer and the rebuild's replay for handovers.
5. **`apps/api`:** the `cash_queue` dataset (cash desk devices only, branch-scoped), push for `H` and `Z` (store, link, review, post), the late link when a bill arrives, `queue-changed` on the event stream; integration tests.
6. **`apps/pos`:**
   - local schema 8; pull of `cash_queue` on desks; `queue-changed` triggers it; `finalizeSale` writes the automatic handover on a desk with an open session
   - the worker: `openSession`, `closeSession`, `confirmHandover` (one transaction each, with the outbox), the local queue view (pulled rows minus local handovers)
   - the **Cash desk** screen: session bar, open and close dialogs, the queue grouped by salesman, `E` correction, the scan field and the not-in-queue confirmation, refunds, the offline banner, `Alt+Q`; i18n keys, logical CSS (rule 10)
7. **E2E** (`e2e/cash-desk.spec.ts`, a counter and a desk in two browser contexts): open a session with a float; a counter's cash bill appears in the desk's queue within 5 s, under its salesman; Enter confirms it and it leaves the queue; a corrected cash received is stored with `corrected_from`; a scan of a queued bill selects it; offline desk: a scan of a bill from a counter that hasn't synced records it from the code, and after both sync the server links it with no mismatch; a second scan is refused; a cash sale on the desk is handed over automatically; close the session.
8. **Docs:** a new **cash desk spec** (`docs/modules/cash-desk/spec.md`: session, queue, scan, payout, the handover document, the desk's keys), sync spec §3 (`cash_queue`), §6 (`H`, `Z`, the new exceptions), §8 (`queue-changed`); POS spec §11 (`Alt+Q`), §13 (the refund code); accounting spec (handovers); data model §6 and §12 (schema 8); posting rules §4.5 (the automatic handover is the desk's own `H`); cash-desk README; decision entry **D-097**.

**Out of scope:** collections, cash-out, the denomination drawer close, the variance and its acknowledgement, the business-day reconciliation and its *missing handovers* list (W1-11); `cash_handover_mode = off` and the server's automatic handovers (CSH-09, Wave 2); voids before the handover and `cash_received_for_void` (W1-12); returns and the first real refund bill (W1-13); the signed QR (D-093); undoing a handover confirmed by mistake (it shows in the drawer variance, W1-11); back-office screens for sessions and handovers (the trace and the exceptions screens, W1-20); printing a handover slip.

## Build notes

- **The bill code already printed and read refunds** (`C03-S000414 -1250`, since W1-06). W1-10 only refuses `-0`; `--450` and `+4320` were already refused and stay as guards.
- **`checkHandover`'s order of problems:** nothing to collect, no session, another cashier's session, not permitted, already handed over (this desk first, then the server's status), a correction with paisa, a correction below the amount due. `buildHandoverDocument` throws the problem as its `DomainError` code.
- **A bill known only from its code** records no cash received or change unless the cashier types the figure; a payout records neither.
- **Session documents** carry `userId` (who opened, or who closed) and `expectedCash` on a close; the float and the expected cash are `null` on the other event.
- **The domain and posting tests came first** (commit `b6d98ad`); the push, pull and desk tests are written with their code, as in W1-09.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| The queue doesn't update within 5 s (push delay, nudge throttling, a slow pull) | Push runs right after finalize (W1-07); `queue-changed` goes only to desks; the e2e and staging measure it; the scan always works as the fallback. |
| A cashier confirms the wrong bill with Enter | Rows grouped by salesman with the amount in large figures; the bill is printed next to the desk to match against; a wrong confirm shows in the drawer variance (W1-11). Undo is not built: documents are immutable (rule 3). |
| An offline scan of a bill whose counter never syncs (a dead PC) | The handover stays unlinked on the server; W1-11's reconciliation and W1-20's exceptions list it; the paper bill is the evidence (PRD risk table). |
| A re-scan or two desks record the same bill twice | Refused on the desk from its local handovers and the queue; on the server stored as `duplicate_handover`, not posted. |
| Pulling `sales_documents` to the desk sends more than it needs | Column-listed (no payload, lines or customer), branch-scoped, cash bills of 7 days: a few hundred rows at Delux. |
| A long server transaction holds `pg_snapshot_xmin` back and the queue lags | The same bounded delay as every pull (rule 8); the scan covers it. |
| Refund payout ships before any refund exists | Golden and integration tests with test-made return rows; W1-13 adds the e2e on a real return. |

## Owner answers (2026-11-01)

The recommended option for each question.

1. **Closing a session in W1-10:** *Close session* without counting, by anyone holding `cash.session.close`: it shows the expected cash (float + handovers − payouts) and lets the next cashier open theirs. The denomination count, the variance and the acknowledgement come with W1-11.
2. **Scanning a bill that isn't in the queue** (the counter hasn't synced, or the desk is offline): scan, then **Enter to confirm** the amount from the code; Esc cancels. A stray scan can't record cash, and a queued bill works the same way (scan selects, Enter confirms).
3. **Refund payouts** are built in this task, tested with test-made refunds, and first used when returns arrive (W1-13), which adds their e2e.
4. **Cash sales on the desk PC itself** are handed over automatically when a session is open on the desk, whoever sells; with no open session they wait in the queue.
