## Goal

**At the end of the day the cashier counts the drawer note by note, the system says how far it is from what should be there, and every rupee of the difference is explained, acknowledged by a manager and traceable to the session and cashier; the next morning the owner opens one report that says whether the day is final and lists every cash bill that never reached the drawer.** In between, the cashier records the other money that moves through the drawer: a mechanic paying his dues (with a printed receipt), and cash going out for tea and petrol, to a supplier's runner or to the bank. Each is a desk document like a handover: local-first, works offline, posted automatically.

## Design

| Choice | Why |
|---|---|
| **Collection** (CSH-05, `Alt+R` on the Cash desk screen, `cash.collection.receive`): search the customer (the same search as `F4`, W1-09), see **the balance** (last synced + this desk's unsynced credit sales − its unsynced collections, *as of 14:05*) and the last collection (ageing comes to the desk with W1-20, owner answer 4); type the amount; pick the method (cash, or any active non-cash method except on account; a method with `requires_reference` needs its reference); *Receive & print (Enter)*. Paying more than the balance is allowed with a warning (*Rs 2,000 more than the balance: kept as an advance*). | PRD W8: "searches for the customer, sees the balance and ageing, records the payment (any method, with reference) and prints a receipt. Works offline." Customers and balances are already on the desk (W1-09); FIFO ageing is built once, for RPT-06. |
| **The collection document** (`CustomerCollectionV1`, family `R`, `D01-R000001`): `id` (UUIDv7), `seq`, `number`, `deviceId`, `branchId`, `sessionId`, `cashierId`, `partyId`, `customerCode`, `customerName` (for the receipt and a reprint), `methodId`, `methodCode`, `amount` (> 0, whole rupees for cash), `reference`, `note`, `balanceBefore` (the desk's figure, printed, never posted), `createdAt`, `businessDate`, `clientVersion`. One local transaction with its outbox row (rule 6), signed after the commit, pushed at once. | Architecture §4.4 gives `R` to collections. A snapshot of the customer's name makes the receipt reprintable offline. |
| **Cash-out** (CSH-06, `Alt+M`, `cash.movement.create`): one dialog, five kinds: **expense** (a category from `expense_categories`: tea, petrol, generator…), **supplier payment** (a supplier, with its balance), **bank deposit** (a bank account of kind `bank`), and **to safe** / **from safe** (owner answer 3). Each: amount, **note (required)**, reference (optional: a slip or voucher number). More than the expected cash in the drawer asks for a second Enter (*Only Rs 3,200 should be in the drawer*): the count may be off, so it warns rather than refuses. | PRD W9: "category, amount, note and an optional reference". A runner leaving with cash must not wait for an argument with the screen. |
| **The cash-out document** (`CashMovementV1`, family `M`, `D01-M000001`): `kind` (`expense` \| `supplier_payment` \| `bank_deposit` \| `to_safe` \| `from_safe`), `amount` (> 0, whole rupees), `expenseCategoryId`, `partyId`, `bankAccountId` (exactly the one its kind needs), `note`, `reference`, and the same identity, session, cashier, time and version fields as `R`. | Architecture §4.4 gives `M` to cash movements. One family, one table per family on the server, like `H`. |
| **The drawer close** (CSH-07, `Alt+X`, `cash.session.close`) replaces W1-10's close without a count: a **denomination grid** (PKR: 5000, 1000, 500, 100, 50, 20, 10 notes; 10, 5, 2, 1 coins, from `DENOMINATIONS` by currency in `packages/domain`), each row *count × note = line total*, the counted total below; **a blind count** (owner answer 2): only after the counted total is entered do the **expected** and **variance** appear (counted − expected, *Short Rs 300* in red, *Over Rs 50* in orange, *Exact* in green). A variance ≠ 0 needs a **reason** (`change_error`, `wrong_bill_confirmed`, `unrecorded_cash_out`, `counterfeit_note`, `unknown`, `other` + note) and a **manager acknowledgement** (owner answer 1): a manager's PIN in the dialog (`cash.variance.approve`, never the session's own cashier, PLT-07), or *Acknowledge later*, which closes anyway and leaves the variance waiting on the day reconciliation. Last, **stays in the drawer** (default: all of the count); the rest is written as a *to safe* cash-out in the same transaction (owner answer 3). *Close & print (Enter)*. | PRD W10.1: "counts the drawer by denomination … The variance is recorded on the cash session. A manager acknowledges it." PRD §4: the cashier can't approve their own variance. A count made without seeing the target is an honest count. |
| **Expected cash** (`expectedCash` in `packages/domain`, one function for the desk, the close and the server's report): **float + handovers in − payouts + cash collections − expenses − supplier payments − bank deposits − to safe + from safe**. Non-cash collections don't count. The desk's own cash sales are in it through their automatic handovers (W1-10). | PRD W10.1, posting rules §4.5. With handover confirmation Required (Delux, D-049) the figure is final on the desk: nothing in it waits for a counter. |
| **The counted close document** is **`CashSessionV2`** (family `Z`): a close adds `countedCash`, `denominations` (`{ "500000": 3, … }`, keys in minor units), `variance`, `varianceReason`, `varianceNote`, `acknowledgedByUserId` (null = later), `leftInDrawer`; an open adds `floatExpected` (what stayed in the drawer at this desk's last close; null for its first session). The open's float is filled in with `floatExpected`; a different float is allowed (the cashier counts what is there). Push keeps accepting `CashSessionV1` (staging desks closed without a count since W1-10): such a close is stored with no count and no variance. | Rule 3: the close is a signed document; adding fields is a new version, never an edit. |
| **Push** (sync spec §6.2) takes `R` and `M` **only from a cash desk** (`wrong_device` otherwise), with the same claim, signature, schema and device checks as `H`; then `reviewCollection` / `reviewCashMovement` / `reviewClose` (`packages/domain`), store and post. **Never refused on business rules** (rule 7). New exceptions: **`cash_not_permitted`** (low) when the server says the cashier lacks the permission, or the acknowledger lacks `cash.variance.approve` or is the session's own cashier; **`float_mismatch`** (medium) when an open's float differs from its `floatExpected`. A collection for a party without a customer account, or a cash-out naming a category, supplier or bank account the server doesn't have: stored, `posting_failed` until fixed (D-095), like a bill with an unmapped method. | One trust path for every desk document (D-097). |
| **On the server** (migration `0012`): **`payments`** (data model §6) holds collections: `direction = 'in'`, `party_id`, `method_id`, `amount`, `reference`, `session_id`, plus the desk's identity, the signed payload and signature, like `cash_handovers`. **`cash_movements`** holds every `M`, **including supplier payments** (with `party_id`): one table per desk family. W1-16 and W1-19 add the back office's bank receipts and payments to `payments` later. **`cash_sessions`** gains `counted_cash, denominations, variance, variance_reason_code, variance_note, left_in_drawer, float_expected, acknowledged_by_user_id, acknowledged_at`. **A later acknowledgement** is `POST /reports/cash-sessions/:id/acknowledge` from the back office (`cash.variance.approve`, not the session's cashier, a closed session with a variance not yet acknowledged): it sets the two acknowledgement fields, audited (owner answer 1). **Role `CASH_SAFE`** goes on 1130 *Cash in safe* in the retail template, and the migration sets it on each tenant's 1130; `validateChartOfAccounts` requires it. Each new table: `tenant_id` + forced RLS, `enable_sync`, `enable_audit`, mirrored in `schema.ts`, no update or delete by the app role except the acknowledgement fields. | Data model §6 had supplier payments in `payments`; keeping each desk family in one table keeps push, idempotency and the session's expected cash simple. The supplier ledger reads `party_ledger_entries`, not `payments`. Recorded in D-098. |
| **Postings** (`packages/posting`, posting rules §4.5), in the accepting transaction under the posting lock, caught up by the worker, replayed by the rebuild check: **collection** cash → `Dr CASH_DRAWER / Cr AR_CONTROL (party)`, non-cash → `Dr <the method's bank account> / Cr AR_CONTROL (party)`, a customer ledger entry of −amount; **expense** → `Dr <the category's account> / Cr CASH_DRAWER`; **supplier payment** → `Dr AP_CONTROL (party) / Cr CASH_DRAWER`, a supplier ledger entry; **bank deposit** → `Dr <the bank's account> / Cr CASH_DRAWER`; **to safe** → `Dr CASH_SAFE / Cr CASH_DRAWER`, **from safe** the reverse; **an open with a float different from `floatExpected`** → the difference between `CASH_SHORT_OVER` and `CASH_DRAWER` (less in the drawer = short); **close** with a variance → `Dr CASH_SHORT_OVER / Cr CASH_DRAWER` (short) or the reverse (over), posted when the close arrives, **whether or not it is acknowledged yet**. Each posts its own amount, so order doesn't matter. | The money moved when the cashier counted it; the acknowledgement is a control, not a posting. With the safe movements and the float check, 1110 equals the cash in the drawer after every count (owner answer 3). |
| **Four desk-only datasets** (sync spec §3; a counter gets `403 not_cash_desk` and leaves them out): `suppliers` (parties with a live supplier account: `id, code, name, phones, city, status`), `supplier_balances` (`party_balances`, role `supplier`: `party_id, balance, updated_at`), `expense_categories` (`id, code, name, is_active`), `bank_accounts` (`id, code, name, kind, is_active`). **Never `gl_account_id`, `legacy` or contact details.** | Like `payment_methods`: the desk names the account, posting maps it. Delux has 35 expense categories and five bank accounts already (legacy import). |
| **Prints** (the desk's default printer and print profile, POS-16): the **collection receipt** (shop header, `D01-R000001`, date and time, customer code and name, amount in words and figures, method and reference, balance before and after *as of the last sync*, cashier; *REPRINT* when reprinted). The **cash-out voucher** (number, kind, category, supplier or bank, amount in words and figures, note, reference, cashier, and a line for the receiver's signature) and the **close slip** (the denomination count, expected, variance and reason, what stayed in the drawer, the cashier, and who acknowledged or *to acknowledge*), owner answer 5. A reprint is logged like a bill's (POS-10); `Alt+P` on the desk lists receipts, vouchers and close slips too. | PRD W8 and the architecture's print table (collection, cash-out, Z report). |
| **Day reconciliation** (CSH-08, back office *Reports → Day reconciliation*, `reports.view`; *Acknowledge* on a waiting variance for `cash.variance.approve`): one branch and business date, computed on request (derived, so `day_reconciliations` isn't stored), CSV export. Sections: **status** *Final* or *Provisional* with the reason (*C04 last heard from 14:05 with 3 bills waiting*; *D01's session is still open*); **sessions** (per session and cashier: float, handovers in, payouts, cash collections, cash-outs by kind, expected, counted, variance, reason, acknowledged by or *waiting*); **missing handovers** by salesman (finalized, not voided cash bills of the day with `handover_status = pending`; refunds not paid out), only when handover confirmation is Required; **unmatched scans** (offline handovers whose bill never arrived); **the day's exceptions** by kind (offline cap, below floor, negative stock, amount mismatch, duplicates…), linked to their documents; **cash in transit** at the end of the day (should be 0, posting rules §4.5). The assembly is a pure `reconcileDay` in `packages/domain`. | PRD W10.2, §3 targets. Computing on request means the report is never stale after a late sync. |
| **Final** = every active device of the branch was heard from after the business day ended (D-056: the next day's 04:00 cutoff) with no unsynced documents from that day and no open sequence gap, and no session of that day is still open. Otherwise *Provisional*, naming each device or session that holds it. | A counter that was offline all day can't be told apart from one that was switched off, so the day waits for it to report. |
| **Cash variance** (RPT-02, *Reports → Cash variance*, `reports.view`): a date range, one row per session (desk, cashier, business date, expected, counted, variance, reason, acknowledged), totals per cashier, CSV export. | RPT-02: "cash variance by session / cashier". |
| **Local schema 9:** the four datasets' tables; `cash_sessions` gains `counted_cash`, `variance`, `left_in_drawer`; collections (`R`) and cash-outs (`M`) in `local_documents` and the outbox like handovers. A desk with open bills, outbox and quarantined rows upgrades in place (rule 9). | Data model §12. |

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| A collection recorded for the wrong customer | The receipt shows the code and name and is handed to the customer; the search shows code, name and phone; the correction is a journal voucher (W1-19). Documents stay immutable (rule 3). |
| A variance hides behind the expected cash shown during the count | A blind count (owner answer 2). Every variance needs a reason and a manager, and stays on the cash variance report. |
| Variances acknowledged *later* pile up | The day reconciliation marks each as *waiting* and the cash variance report lists them until a manager acknowledges. |
| The float typed at open differs from what stayed | Allowed (the cashier counts what is there), posted to 6500 and flagged `float_mismatch`, so 1110 stays true. |
| A cash-out larger than the drawer | A warning with a second Enter; the variance at close shows the truth. |
| The day stays provisional because a counter is switched off for days | The report names the device and when it was last heard from; a revoked device no longer counts (it is not active). |
| Closes from W1-10's staging desks have no count | `CashSessionV1` stays accepted and shows as *not counted* on the reports. |
| The task is bigger than its 3 days | One task (owner answer 5); the build order puts the desk documents and the close (which the pilot needs first) before the reports. |
| A non-cash collection on a method not mapped to a bank | Stored and `posting_failed` until the method is mapped (D-095), like a bill. |

## Owner answers (2026-11-02)

The recommended option for each question.

1. **Acknowledging a variance:** a manager enters their PIN in the close dialog; if none is there, *Acknowledge later* closes anyway and the variance waits, marked, on the day reconciliation, where a manager acknowledges it in the back office (audited). Never the session's own cashier.
2. **The count is blind:** the expected cash and the variance appear only after the counted total is entered.
3. **Cash at the end of the day:** the close asks how much stays in the drawer (default: all); the rest is a *to safe* movement (`Dr 1130 Cash in safe / Cr 1110 Cash drawer`, role `CASH_SAFE`). The next open's float is filled in with what stayed; a different float posts the difference to 6500 Cash short/over and is flagged `float_mismatch`. Cash-out also has *To safe* and *From safe*.
4. **Customer ageing at the desk:** the balance and the last collection now; ageing comes to the desk with W1-20, which builds it for RPT-06.
5. **Prints and size:** the collection receipt, a cash-out voucher with a signature line, and the close slip; W1-11 stays one task of about 5 days, built in the order desk documents → close → reports.
