## Goal

**A salesman can park a bill while the customer fetches the old part, serve the next customer, and pick the first bill up again exactly as it was; and a wrong bill caught before its cash reaches the desk is voided with a manager's PIN and a reason, keeps its number, prints as VOID, drops off the cash desk's queue within seconds, is reversed in the books, and, if asked, is reissued as a new bill linked to it.** Nothing is ever edited (rule 3, D-002): a void is its own signed document, and the corrected bill is a new one.

## Design

| Choice | Why |
|---|---|
| **Hold** (POS-08, `F8`, `pos.sale.hold`): moves the person's open bill (lines, prices with their list and floor snapshots, approvals, customer, tier, walk-in phone) into `held_bills` with an optional **label** (*Blue Suzuki, waiting for old part*, 40 characters) and leaves an empty bill. *Bill held · Shift+F8 to recall*. Refused on an empty bill and with **20 bills held** on the counter (*Recall or clear a held bill first*). | PRD W1: "hold / park bills and resume them on the same device". A cap keeps a forgotten pile from growing all day. |
| **Recall** (`Shift+F8`): this counter's held bills, newest first: held at, by whom, label, customer, items, total; `↑ ↓` and **Enter** recall, **`Del`** clears one (after a confirm). On a bill with lines, Enter asks *Hold this bill and recall C…?* and a second Enter swaps them in **one local transaction**. **Which bills each person sees: owner question 4.** | One list, keyboard only (POS-03). A swap that can't lose a bill halfway. |
| **A held bill comes back as it was:** a later pull doesn't change it (like the open bill, POS spec §10); approvals hold while the prices stay (§10); the credit check runs at payment, on fresh figures (§15). **Never pushed** (architecture §4.7, data model §5). | The customer was quoted those prices minutes ago. Held bills are a counter convenience, not a document. |
| **Expiry** (PRD: "held bills expire at day close"): a held bill expires when **its business day is over** (the next day's 04:00 cutoff, D-056), checked at start, after each unlock and with the minute timer; expired bills are deleted and the count written to `diagnostic_log` (no lines, no customer). **Owner question 4.** | One deterministic rule every counter computes alone, offline. |
| **Void** (POS-11, `Alt+V` *Void a bill*): a list of **this counter's bills of today** that aren't voided, filtered as the number is typed or by **scanning the bill's code** (the customer's paper bill). The dialog shows the bill (number, time, salesman, customer, total, payments) and its **cash desk status**: *Not handed over yet*, *Paid without cash*, *Handed over at 14:05 by Ali: use a return* (refused), or *Offline: the cash desk can't be checked* (a warning, allowed). Then a **reason** (owner question 5) with a note for *Other*, and the **approver**: name and **their own PIN**, checked offline like a price approval (POS spec §10: wrong PINs count towards the approver's own lock). *Void & print (Enter)*. | PRD W5: "a Manager or Cashier with the permission voids it, with a PIN and a reason". PLT-07: the approval is on the same screen, so a salesman starts it and the manager walks over. |
| **Who may void** (`canVoid` in `packages/domain`, used by the counter and the server): the approver holds **`pos.void`** in this branch and their role's **`voidWindow`** covers the bill: `none` never; `same_day_before_handover` only on the bill's business date and before its cash is handed over. **The approver may be the person voiding** if they hold the permission, still with their PIN and a reason (as for prices, owner answer 2 to W1-05). **After the handover: owner question 1** (recommended: never, for every role; migration 0013 moves the Manager and Owner templates and roles from `same_day` to `same_day_before_handover`). | D-002, PRD W5 and Q4: "until the business day closes and before the cash handover. After that, use a return." Posting rules §4.4 relies on it: the reversal itself clears cash in transit. |
| **Where a bill is voided: on the device that made it** (owner question 2): it holds the bill with its lines for 30 days (`local_documents`), so it can print the VOID copy and start the reissue offline. A bill made on the desk is voided on the desk. | Other counters don't hold another counter's lines until W1-13 pulls the branch's 90-day bills. |
| **The live check of the cash desk status** when online: `GET /api/sync/bills/status?number=C03-S000412` (device token, only the device's own bills) → `{ known, status, handoverStatus, handedOverAt, cashierName }`, looking at the bill **and at any handover of that number**, also one scanned offline whose bill hadn't arrived. At most 3 s; offline or no answer is *unknown*. The desk knows it from its own queue and handovers, so it doesn't ask. | A void needs a fresh answer and happens a few times a day; the pulled queue is desk-only and can be minutes old. Offline, the server still catches it (below). |
| **The void document** (`SaleVoidV1`, **a new family `V`**, `C03-V000001`): `id` (UUIDv7), `seq`, `number`, `deviceId`, `branchId`, `saleId`, `saleNumber`, `saleBusinessDate`, `saleTotal`, `saleCashDue`, `userId` (who voided), `approvedBy`, `reason`, `note`, `handoverSeen` (`pending` \| `not_applicable` \| `unknown`: what the counter knew), `createdAt`, `businessDate`, `clientVersion`. Written **in one local transaction with its outbox row** (rule 6), signed after the commit, printed, pushed at once. | The bill is immutable and already signed (rule 3, D-082): the void can't be a change to it. A family of its own keeps the `S` series gapless for bills (POS-13), and push, idempotency and gap detection work as for every family (rule 7). Recorded in the decision entry (D-099). |
| **The VOID print:** the voided bill reprinted with a **VOID** band: voided at, by, approved by, reason, and the void's number. A later reprint of a voided bill carries it too. The customer's paper bill is taken back. | PRD W5: "the voided bill keeps its number and prints as VOID". |
| **Reissue** (optional): after the void, *Reissue now (Enter)* or *Done (Esc)*. Reissue loads the voided bill's customer, tier, lines and prices into the open bill (an open bill must be empty first: hold it, `F8`), marked *Reissue of C03-S000412*; it is then corrected and paid like any bill and gets the next `S` number. **Approvals: owner question 3.** The sale document carries **`reissueOf: { id, number }`** (optional, additive to `SaleDocumentV1`, like W1-09's fields), and the printed bill says *Reissue of C03-S000412*. | PRD W5: "optionally issues a corrected bill linked to it". Starting from the voided lines is the fast path (PRD risk table: "fast void/reissue"). |
| **On the counter, a voided bill no longer counts:** not in the credit check's `unsynced` or `offlineToday` (POS spec §15), nor in the desk's unsynced on-account balance (cash desk spec §8). The reprint list marks it *VOID*. | A voided on-account bill must not block the customer's next sale. |
| **On the desk:** the queue already hides voided bills (cash desk spec §3), and the server's void changes the bill's row, so `queue-changed` drops it from every desk within seconds (sync spec §8). **Scanning a voided bill's code** is refused with *C03-S000412 was voided at 14:10: don't take cash for it* (`checkHandover` gains `bill_voided`). Offline, the desk can't know; the server flags it (below). | D-059: cash taken for a voided bill is a possible leak. |
| **Push of `V`** (sync spec §6.2) from **any kind of device**, with the same claim, signature and schema checks; `wrong_device` when the bill's number isn't the pushing device's (owner question 2); `unknown_reference` for an unknown voider or approver. **A void whose bill hasn't arrived waits** and is applied when the bill is accepted, like a handover (W1-10), under the same per-bill lock. Then `reviewVoid` (`packages/domain`) judges it against the server's roles and the bill, and the bill becomes `voided` (status, `void_document_id`, `void_reason_code`, `voided_at`, `voided_by_user_id`, `void_approved_by_user_id`: workflow fields, audited, data model §5). **Never refused on business rules** (rule 7); findings are exceptions: | One trust path for every device document. A void is a fact once the counter printed it: refusing it would leave the counter and the server disagreeing. |
| — **`void_not_permitted`** (medium): the server says the approver lacks `pos.void`, or the void is outside their window (`details.cause`: `not_permitted`, `window_none`, `other_day`, `after_handover`). The void still stands. | D-011. |
| — **`cash_received_for_void`** (high, D-059, already in the exception kinds since 0008): the bill has a handover `in`, whichever arrived first; the handover is still posted (the cash is in the drawer), so the day reconciliation shows it in cash in transit until it is explained. | The two leaks D-059 names only become visible after sync. |
| — **`duplicate_void`** (medium): a second void of the same bill: stored with `duplicate_of_id`, never posted. | Like `duplicate_handover`. |
| **The void's approval is also an `overrides` row** of kind `void` on the bill (data model §5), so the overrides report (RPT-03, W1-20) sees it. **Audit:** `sale.voided` with the device, the voider and the approver (PLT-08). | |
| **The reversal** (`postVoid`, `packages/posting`, posting rules §4.4), in the accepting transaction under the posting lock, caught up by the worker, replayed by the rebuild check (source `sales_void`): **every line of the sale's journal entry swapped**, dated the void's business date; its **stock movements reversed at the same unit costs** (back into SHOP); its **party ledger entry reversed**. It takes the sale's own posting, so a bill not yet posted (`posting_failed`) holds its void back until both post, in order. | Posting rules §4.4; rule 4: derived and rebuildable. |
| **Server storage** (migration `0013`): **`sales_voids`** (the `V` documents: identity, `sale_document_id` (null until the bill arrives), `sale_number`, voider, approver, reason, note, `handover_seen`, `business_date`, the signed payload, hash and signature, `exception_count`, `duplicate_of_id`), like `cash_handovers`; on **`sales_documents`** the void fields above and `reissue_of_id`, `reissue_of_number` (from the document, set once at push); the exception kinds `void_not_permitted`, `duplicate_void`; the posting source `sales_void`; and owner question 1's role change, **tenant by tenant** (the lesson of 0012's `CASH_SAFE`). Each new table: `tenant_id` + forced RLS, `enable_audit`, mirrored in `schema.ts`; the app role never updates or deletes a void. | Data model §5 already names the void fields and `reissue_of_id`. |
| **Local schema 10:** `held_bills` (`id`, `user_id`, `held_at`, `business_date`, `label`, `bill`: the open bill as JSON, with its version); voids (`V`) in `local_documents` and the outbox like any document, with an index on `$.saleId`. A counter with open bills, held bills, outbox and quarantined rows upgrades in place (rule 9). | Data model §12 already lists `held_bills`. |

## Scope

1. **`packages/domain`:** `hold.ts` (`checkHold`, `heldExpired`), `void.ts` (`canVoid`, `checkVoid`, `buildVoidDocument`, `reviewVoid`), `reissueBill` (the open bill from a voided document), `checkHandover`'s `bill_voided`, the void reasons; golden `hold.json`, `void.json`, `void-review.json`, `reissue.json`; the role templates (owner question 1).
2. **`packages/contracts`:** `SaleVoidV1`, family `V` in push, `reissueOf` on `SaleDocumentV1`, the exception kinds, the bill status answer.
3. **`packages/db`:** migration `0013_void_reissue.sql`, `schema.ts`.
4. **`packages/posting`:** `postVoid` and its golden cases, the database layer, `postPending` and the rebuild's replay with the new source.
5. **`apps/api`:** push for `V` (wait for the bill, review, store, set the bill voided, post, audit), the reissue link on a sale, `cash_received_for_void` when a handover meets a voided bill and when a void meets a handed-over bill, `GET /api/sync/bills/status`; integration tests.
6. **`apps/pos`:** local schema 10; the worker's `holdBill`, `recallBill` (the swap), `clearHeld`, the expiry sweep, `voidBill` (the void, its outbox row, one transaction), the reissue into the open bill; the Hold and Recall dialogs (`F8`, `Shift+F8`), the Void dialog (`Alt+V`, the list, the scan, the status, the reason, the approver's PIN), the *Reissue now* step; the VOID band on the print and the reprint; *Reissue of…* on the bill; voided bills out of the credit figures; the desk's `bill_voided` on a scan; the key bar and POS spec §11; i18n keys, logical CSS (rule 10).
7. **E2E** (`e2e/counter.spec.ts`, `e2e/till.spec.ts`): hold a bill, sell another, lock and unlock, recall the first as it was; recall with a bill open swaps them; a salesman voids a cash bill with the manager's PIN and a reason, the VOID copy prints, the bill leaves the desk's queue within 5 s, and a reissue at a corrected price joins the queue; a handed-over bill can't be voided online; a void made offline syncs on reconnect, and a desk that took the cash offline meanwhile shows `cash_received_for_void` on the day reconciliation.
8. **Docs:** POS spec (a new §17 Hold, §18 Void and reissue; §11 the keys; §13 the VOID band; §15 voided bills in the figures), sync spec §6.1 (`reissueOf`) and §6.2 (`V`, the exceptions, the bill status call), cash desk spec (§5 the voided scan, §13 the exception, §14), architecture §4.4 (family `V`), data model §2, §5, §10, §12, posting rules §4.4 (dated the void's business date, the wait for an unposted bill), accounting spec, pos README, decision entry **D-099** (and the change to the void window if owner question 1 is answered as recommended).

**Out of scope:** a void after the handover with the cash paid back at the desk (owner question 1, option B); voiding another counter's bill (owner question 2, option B); returns and exchanges, which replace a void after the handover or the day (W1-13); the voids and reissues report (RPT-04) and the exceptions screen (W1-20); resuming a held bill on another counter (architecture §4.7: not in the MVP); a held bill surviving its business day; editing role limits in the back office.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| The cash desk takes the cash for a bill the counter is voiding offline | The live check online; offline, the dialog warns and `cash_received_for_void` (high) puts it on the day reconciliation, with cash in transit off by that amount until explained (D-059). |
| A reissue is made but its void never reaches the server (a quarantined void) | The void and its reissue sit in the same outbox; a refused void shows under *Needs attention* on the counter (POS spec §14); the voids and reissues report (W1-20) lists reissues whose original isn't voided. |
| Managers void instead of doing returns, to hide sales | Every void needs a PIN and a reason, is audited and an override, and the window ends at the handover; RPT-04 (W1-20) lists them by approver. |
| A desk's own cash bill can't be voided (it was handed over automatically) | Expected with owner question 1's recommended answer: use a return (W1-13). Option B would cover it. |
| Held bills pile up or hold stale prices | At most 20; they expire with their business day; prices are the ones quoted, by design. |
| The void reaches the server before its bill | It waits and applies when the bill arrives, under the per-bill lock (like an offline scan). |
| The task is twice its 2 days | Hold, then void on the counter, then the server, so the pilot gets hold even if the void slips; the size goes to the owner (question 5). |

## Questions for the owner

Each has a recommended option first.

1. **A void after the cash handover:**
   - **A (recommended):** never, for any role, as D-002, PRD W5 and posting rules §4.4 say: after the handover, it's a return (W1-13). The Manager and Owner templates (now `same_day`) and every role copied from them move to `same_day_before_handover`. A desk's own cash bill is handed over at once, so it is corrected with a return too.
   - **B:** a Manager (window `same_day`) may void after the handover on the same day; the voided bill then shows in the desk's queue as *Pay back Rs X*, paid out like a refund. About one more day, and a new decision against D-002.
2. **Where a bill can be voided:**
   - **A (recommended):** only on the device that made it (it has the lines offline, for the VOID print and the reissue).
   - **B:** on any device of the branch while online (the bill fetched from the server; offline only on its own device).
3. **The reissue's approvals:**
   - **A (recommended):** kept from the voided bill: they hold while a line's price stays at or above what was approved (POS spec §10); a lower price asks again.
   - **B:** every approval asked again on the reissue.
4. **Held bills, who and how long:**
   - **A (recommended):** each person recalls only the bills they held (like the open bill); a held bill expires at the end of its business day (04:00).
   - **B:** anyone on the counter sees and recalls every held bill (shown with who held it); whoever finalizes is the seller.
   - **C:** as A, but expiring at the shop's closing time (20:00).
5. **Void reasons and size:** the reasons *Wrong item*, *Wrong quantity*, *Wrong price or discount*, *Wrong customer or payment*, *Duplicate bill*, *Customer cancelled*, *Other* (with a note); and W1-12 stays one task of about 4 days, built in the order hold → void on the counter → server.
