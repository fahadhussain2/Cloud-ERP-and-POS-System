## Goal

**A salesman picks the customer in two keystrokes and sells on account without a phone call, offline too, and the shop can't be talked past a limit without a manager.** `F4` finds a customer by name, phone or code; their tier sets the prices; the bill shows their balance and what is left of their limit. *On account* is enabled for credit customers. The counter checks the **limit** against the last pulled balance plus this counter's sales the server hasn't counted yet, and, while offline, the **offline daily cap** (Rs 25,000 at Delux). Over either needs a manager's PIN, recorded on the bill. A walk-in can leave a phone number so the bill can be found later. The server re-checks every on-account sale with what it knows from all counters and flags breaches, never refusing one (D-011).

## Design

| Choice | Why |
|---|---|
| **Three new pull datasets** (sync spec §3), tenant-scoped: **`customers`** (table `parties`, only parties that have a live customer account): `id, code, name, phones, city, status`; **`customer_accounts`**: `id, party_id, price_tier_id, credit_allowed, credit_limit, offline_daily_cap`; **`customer_balances`** (table `party_balances`, `role = 'customer'`): `party_id, balance, updated_at`. **Never** `email, address, contact_person, tax_id, notes, label_abbr, legacy, payment_terms_days`. Delux: 131 customers, so the first pull adds a fraction of a second. | SYN-01: "customers (with balance snapshot and limits)". The counter gets only what selling needs; addresses, tax numbers and notes stay on the server. |
| **A party that becomes a customer later is still sent:** migration `0010` adds a trigger on `customer_accounts` (insert, and a soft-deleted account restored) that touches its `parties` row, so the row gets a new `txid` and the filtered `customers` pull returns it. A customer whose account is removed arrives as an account tombstone; the counter hides parties without a live account. `parties`, `customer_accounts` and `party_balances` get the SSE nudge triggers (`erp.enable_sync_notify`). | The pull cursor is per table (rule 8); a filter alone would miss a supplier who starts buying on credit. |
| **The balance snapshot** is `party_balances.balance` as pulled (positive = the customer owes, negative = an advance; no row = 0). Opening balances come with W1-14; until then a customer's balance is only what they bought on account since go-live. The customer dialog shows *Balance Rs 45,000 · as of 14:32* (the last successful pull). | Data model §3. The figure the salesman sees says how fresh it is. |
| **What this counter adds to the balance** (`unsynced`): the on-account part of every bill of this counter for that customer **that the pulled balance can't include yet**: still in the outbox (pending or refused), or accepted by the server **after the last complete pull started**. Local schema 7 records `accepted_at` on `local_documents` when push acks a bill, and `sync_state` the start time of the last pull that ran to `hasMore = false`. | PRD W2: "the last-synced balance plus this counter's unsynced credit sales". Without the second half, a bill acked a second before a pull that started earlier would be counted in neither, for a moment. |
| **Offline** = what the status bar shows at finalize: *Offline* (the browser has no network, or the counter hasn't reached the server since the network came back, POS spec §7). **The offline daily cap counts this counter's on-account sales to that customer made while offline on this business date** (D-056), plus this bill. Online, only the limit applies (owner answer 1). | The glossary's meaning: the most a customer can buy on account per day from an offline counter. Counters can't see each other offline, so the cap is per counter. |
| **The rule is pure, in `packages/domain`** (`credit.ts`, rule 2): `checkCredit({ account, balance, unsynced, offline, offlineToday, amount })` → `{ allowed, needs: ('credit_limit' \| 'offline_cap')[], available, capLeft }`. Over the limit = `balance + unsynced + amount > credit_limit` (**exactly at the limit is fine**; `credit_limit` null = no limit, D-085). Over the cap = `offline && offlineToday + amount > offline_daily_cap` (a cap of 0 means every offline credit sale needs a PIN). `credit_allowed` false or no account → on account isn't offered at all. Golden cases first. | CLAUDE.md rule 2 and the checkout-rules checklist: normal, exactly at the limit and cap, over with and without override, a null limit, an advance balance. |
| **Customer on the bill:** `Bill` gains `customerId` and `walkinPhone` (both null = walk-in; an open bill saved before W1-09 reads as walk-in). Choosing a customer **sets the bill's tier to theirs** (`customer_accounts.price_tier_id`) with the existing tier change (POS spec §10: lines at list move, bargained lines are checked again). **Back to walk-in puts the bill on the default tier** (owner answer 3). `F7` can still change the tier afterwards. | PRD W1 step 3 and CAT-04: "a customer's tier applies automatically". A mechanic's price shouldn't stay on a walk-in's bill by accident. |
| **`F4` *Customer* dialog** (POS-05): one search box; results from an in-memory list in the worker, like item search: a **code** equal or starting; **phone** digits (4 or more) found anywhere in a normalized number; every word starts a word of the name or city. Each result: code, name, city, the phone's last four digits (`•••• 4567`), tier, and for credit customers *Balance · Limit · Available*. `↑ ↓`, Enter picks; `Esc` cancels. **Walk-in** is the first row, with an optional *Walk-in phone* field. `F4` on a bill with a customer opens the dialog on them, with *Back to walk-in*. Inactive customers aren't listed. | Fast pick by name, phone or code, keyboard only (POS-03, D-053). The full number isn't shown on a screen the customer can see (owner answer 4). |
| **Phone rule** (`normalizePhone`, `packages/domain`): keep digits; a leading `+92`, `0092` or `92` before 10 digits becomes `0`; valid = 7 to 15 digits. Used for the walk-in phone, the customer search and later the bill lookup without a receipt (W1-13). The walk-in phone is stored normalized on the document. | One rule for typed numbers and imported ones (`0300-1234567`, `+92 300 1234567` are the same number). |
| **The bill panel's context strip** shows the customer: *Khan Motors (C0042) · Mechanic prices · Balance Rs 45,000 · Available Rs 55,000* (*No limit* when null), or *Walk-in · 0300•••4567*. | The salesman sees the credit position before taking payment. |
| **On account in the payment pad:** enabled when the bill has a customer with `credit_allowed` and the seller holds `pos.credit.sell`; otherwise disabled with its reason as a hint (*Choose a credit customer* / *Your role can't sell on account*). The **On account popup** shows the total, the balance, this counter's unsynced amount, *after this bill*, the limit, and offline the cap used today. **Split payment** may include an on-account row (part cash, part credit); the check uses the on-account part. | PRD W2. Split with credit is common for mechanics paying part now. |
| **Over the limit or the cap:** the popup says which and by how much, and *Finalize* opens the approval on the same screen (PLT-07 pattern, POS spec §10): an approver with **`pos.credit.over_limit`** for this branch picks their name and enters **their own PIN**, then a reason (*Customer will pay soon, Regular customer, Owner's instruction*, or *Other* with a note; owner answer 2). The seller may approve themselves if they hold it. Both breaches at once need one approval that names both. The approval is taken **at finalize**, for this amount, and isn't kept on the open bill: any change to the bill asks again. | D-013: "over the limit needs a manager PIN". The figures it approves only exist once the tender is known. |
| **The sale document stays v1, with additive fields** (`SaleDocumentV1`): `customerId` and `walkinPhone` are now filled (`walkinPhone` only without a customer); `overrides[].kind` gains `credit_limit` and `offline_cap` (already in the server's `overrides` check), `lineNo` null, `values` `{ limit, balance, unsynced, amount }` or `{ cap, offlineToday, amount }`; and an optional **`credit`** block on bills with an on-account payment: `{ offline, balance, balanceAsOf, unsynced, offlineToday }`, the counter's view when it decided. Bills already in outboxes still parse. The server deploys before the counters (same release). | Rule 7: the document carries what the server needs to judge it later. A v2 schema for optional fields would be churn before go-live; the server keeps accepting every v1 already written. |
| **The server re-checks credit** (`reviewCredit`, `packages/domain`, the counter's own rule): in the push transaction, before posting, the tenant's posting order lock is taken (`holdPostingOrder`, the lock every posting takes first, so no deadlock with the catch-up) and the customer's balance read under it, then **`credit_limit_exceeded`** (medium) when the server's balance + the on-account amount is over the limit without a covering `credit_limit` approval from someone the server says holds `pos.credit.over_limit`; **`offline_cap_exceeded`** (medium) when an offline bill takes that device's offline on-account total for the customer on that business date over the cap without an `offline_cap` approval. `details.cause`: `no_approval`, `not_permitted`, `credit_not_allowed` (no account, or credit not allowed), `seller_not_permitted` (seller lacks `pos.credit.sell`), with both the server's and the counter's figures. A `customerId` that isn't this tenant's party is `unknown_reference` (as for items). | Architecture: "each counter applies the limit plus the offline cap; breaches are flagged at sync". The server sees every counter's sales, so it finds the breach three offline counters make together. Never a refusal (D-011). |
| **Posting is unchanged:** W1-08 already posts the on-account part to `AR_CONTROL` with the party and writes the customer's ledger and balance, due after their terms. The new balance reaches the counters on the next pull (the nudge makes that seconds). | Built and tested in W1-08 with a hand-made document; W1-09 makes the counter produce it. |
| **The printed bill** shows the customer's name and code instead of *Walk-in*, and *On account* as a payment row. No balance or limit on paper; the walk-in phone isn't printed. | PRD W2: "the receipt shows On account". The bill may be handed to someone other than the account holder. |
| **Local schema 7:** `customers`, `customer_accounts`, `customer_balances` (exactly the pulled columns), `local_documents.accepted_at`, and the pull start time in `sync_state`. An existing counter with open bills, outbox rows and quarantined bills upgrades in place (rule 9). | Data model §12. |
| **Personal data:** customers' names and phones are now stored on counter PCs (OPFS). They're never written to `diagnostic_log`, the server logs or Sentry; only the party id is. | Customers' contacts are personal data; the counter needs them offline to search, nothing else does. |

## Scope

1. **`packages/domain`:** `credit.ts` (`checkCredit`, `reviewCredit`), `phone.ts` (`normalizePhone`), `Bill.customerId` / `walkinPhone`, `buildSaleDocument` fills the customer, walk-in phone, credit block and credit overrides, `checkTender`'s `hasCreditCustomer` from the account; the credit reasons; golden `credit.json`, `credit-review.json`, `phone.json`, new cases in `sale-document.json` and `tender.json`.
2. **`packages/contracts`:** `SaleDocumentV1` additions (credit kinds and values, `credit`), `CUSTOMER_COLUMNS` and the three datasets in `PULL_DATASETS`.
3. **`packages/db`:** migration `0010_customers_on_counter.sql` (the touch trigger, the nudge triggers on `parties`, `customer_accounts`, `party_balances`), `schema.ts` if anything changes.
4. **`apps/api`:** the three pull datasets (column-listed, filtered); push loads the account, balance and the device's offline total for the day, runs `reviewCredit`, stores the exceptions; integration tests.
5. **`apps/pos`:**
   - local schema 7, pull applies the three datasets, the worker's customer list and `searchCustomers`, `creditFigures(customerId)` (balance, as of, unsynced, offline today), `accepted_at` on ack, the pull start time
   - `F4` and the *Customer* dialog, the walk-in phone, the tier change on pick and on back to walk-in, the context strip
   - *On account* in the payment pad, the On account popup, the on-account row in Split, the credit approval dialog (reusing the approval pieces), the customer and *On account* on the printed bill
6. **E2E** (`e2e/counter.spec.ts`): pick a customer by phone → their tier's prices; a credit sale within the limit, no PIN; a sale over the limit refused without and finalized with a manager's PIN; part cash, part on account; offline: a sale under the cap without a PIN and the next one over the cap with one; a walk-in with a phone; the printed bill shows the customer and *On account*.
7. **`tools/legacy-import`** (owner answer 5): the mapping file gains `partyCredit` by party code (`creditAllowed`, `creditLimit` in rupees, `offlineDailyCap` in rupees, each optional; the defaults stay: credit allowed, no limit, Rs 25,000), validated like `partyTiers`, and the load writes them to `customer_accounts`. The review report shows each credit customer's terms. A re-run with a changed entry updates the account; without an entry it keeps the defaults.
8. **Docs:** POS spec §10 (customer and tier), §11 (`F4`), new §15 *Customers and credit*; sync spec §3 (the datasets), §6.1 (customer, walk-in phone, credit block, credit overrides), §6.2 (the credit exceptions); data model §12 (schema 7); POS README; decision entry **D-096**.

**Out of scope:** editing customers, limits, tiers and caps in the back office (no Wave 1 task had it; the mapping file sets them for the pilot, owner answer 5; added later by W1-22, D-109); customer collections and receipts (W1-11, CSH-05); opening balances (W1-14); finding a bill by the walk-in phone (W1-13); the outstanding and ageing report and the exceptions screen (W1-20); stock on the counter; adding a new customer at the counter.

## Build notes (2026-10-31)

- **The customer lives on the counter's open bill** (`OpenBill.customer`, `walkinPhone`), not on the domain `Bill`: `buildSaleDocument` takes `customer`, the counter's `credit` view and a `creditApproval` instead of `hasCreditCustomer`. Adding fields to `Bill` would have rewritten about 50 bill golden cases for nothing the rules use.
- **The server's lock:** push takes the tenant's posting order lock (`holdPostingOrder`, now shared with `postDocument`) before reading the balance, instead of `FOR UPDATE` on the balance row. The catch-up takes the posting lock first and then writes the balance, so locking the balance first could deadlock with it.
- **Causes:** the credit exceptions use `no_approval` for a missing approval, like `below_floor_no_override`.
- **`reviewSale` skips credit overrides:** a new golden case caught one being read as a bill-discount approval (it has no line, like the bill discount's).
- **The approval dialog takes its reasons:** the price reasons by default, the credit reasons (owner answer 2) for credit; the approver needs `pos.credit.over_limit` (`checkCreditApprover`).
- **Customer search** runs on the screen over the pulled list (`searchCustomers`), not in the worker: a shop has a few hundred customers at most.
- **Pull and push tests were written with the code**, not before it, for the API side (scope 4); the domain, contract and importer tests came first.
- **A bill can be counted twice for a few seconds** when a pull that began before its acceptance already read its posted balance: the conservative side (an approval asked for too early); the next pull, at once with the nudge, corrects it.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| A bill acked just before a pull whose snapshot doesn't include it yet (a long server transaction holds `pg_snapshot_xmin` back) is briefly counted in neither figure | Rare and short (seconds); the server's re-check flags any breach it causes. |
| Three offline counters each allow Rs 25,000 to one customer | The cap is per counter by nature; the server flags the day's total (`offline_cap_exceeded` per device, `credit_limit_exceeded` on the balance). Delux runs 3 counters, so the worst case is Rs 75,000 a day, reviewed the next morning. |
| Migrated customers have no limit (D-085), so only the cap protects until the owner sets limits | The review report lists every credit customer with their terms; the owner sets limits in the mapping file and the importer re-run applies them (owner answer 5). |
| Customers' phones on counter PCs | Only the columns selling needs; masked on screen; never in logs; the PCs are the shop's own, behind the PIN. |
| Changing `SaleDocumentV1` additively while counters hold W1-08 bills | Every new field is optional or a widened enum; a test parses a W1-08 document; the server ships first. |
| The tier jump on pick surprises a salesman mid-bill | It uses the existing tier change: bargained lines keep their price and are checked again, as with `F7`. |

## Owner answers (2026-10-31)

The recommended option for each question.

1. **Offline cap:** it counts the on-account sales to that customer **made while this counter is offline, on this business day**, plus the bill being sold. Once the counter is back online, those sales are in the balance and the limit covers them.
2. **Reasons for going over the limit:** *Customer will pay soon, Regular customer, Owner's instruction*, or *Other* with a note.
3. **Back to walk-in** returns the bill to the default tier (Retail), so a mechanic's price doesn't stay on a walk-in bill.
4. **Phones on the counter screen:** only the last four digits are shown (`•••• 4567`); the search still works on the full number.
5. **Limits, tiers and caps before go-live:** set through the legacy mapping file and the importer re-run for the pilot (scope 7); a back-office *Customers* screen is not in Wave 1.
6. **The server's credit re-check** is built in this task (the pilot sells on account at three counters).
